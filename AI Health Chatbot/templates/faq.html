Emergency Guidance & Red Flags

If you or someone around you experiences any of the following red flag medical symptoms, do not rely on a chatbot or search engine. Seek emergency hospital care or dial emergency hotlines immediately.

Chest Pain / Heart

Crushing pressure radiating to the left arm, neck, or jaw, accompanied by cold sweat or nausea.

Severe Breathlessness

Gasping for air, blue tint on lips or fingernails, or inability to speak full sentences.

Stroke (F.A.S.T.)

Face drooping, Arm weakness, Slurred speech, Time to call 112 immediately.

Uncontrolled Bleeding

Spurting or heavy continuous blood loss that does not cease after direct firm pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this chatbot provide clinical medical diagnoses?

No. HealthAware AI is designed exclusively for public-health awareness, preventive lifestyle guidance, and general disease education. It does not replace the diagnosis, prescription, or clinical assessment of a qualified healthcare practitioner.

What happens if the AI model is not confident in my question?

The system implements a configurable confidence threshold (default 60%). If the model's confidence falls below this threshold, it automatically delivers a clinical safety fallback: informing the user that the query could not be reliably matched and recommending consulting a doctor or rephrasing the question.

How do the ML and Deep Learning models differ?

The Machine Learning engine uses TF-IDF n-gram vectorization with Logistic Regression. It is lightweight, ultra-fast, and highly accurate on common medical keywords. The Deep Learning model uses a Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) neural network with word embeddings, allowing it to capture forward and backward sequence dependencies in conversational phrasing.

Can I query the bot in Kannada?

Yes! The NLP preprocessing pipeline incorporates bilingual transliteration mapping for Kannada health terms such as jvara (fever), seetha (cold), kemmu (cough), sakkare kayile (diabetes), and rakta ottada (hypertension).

How does speech input and text-to-speech work?

You can click the microphone icon in the chat interface to dictate your query using your device's Web Speech Recognition API. Clicking the speaker button on any bot response plays the guidance aloud via the Web SpeechSynthesis API.
